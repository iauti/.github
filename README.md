## About Us

IAUTI Labs is a boutique **mobile & web product studio** based in Vienna, Austria. We partner with startups and product teams to design, develop, and ship high-quality mobile and web products — from idea to App Store and beyond.

We care deeply about engineering craftsmanship: clean architecture, reliable CI/CD, meaningful analytics, and code that teams can actually maintain and grow.
